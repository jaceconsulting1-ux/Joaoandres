## About us

JACE Consulting is a water and soil consulting initiative focused on hydrological, geospatial and environmental analysis.

We combine modelling, spatial information and data analysis to support planning, applied research and natural resource management.

This is the institutional portfolio of **JACE Consulting**. It brings together the team’s capabilities, consulting services and members’ research and consulting experience, preserving each project’s attribution.

## Services

| Service | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Environmental services governance | Ecosystem service management and valuation, stakeholder participation and environmental governance. |
| Socio-environmental data analysis | Statistics, geospatial analysis and indicators to understand territories and their social dynamics. |
| Interactive platform development | Map viewers, dashboards and applications for exploring and communicating technical information. |
| Water infrastructure and sanitation planning | Hydrological and hydraulic studies, drainage, water supply and water treatment alternatives. |
| Spatial planning and regulatory analysis | Environmental zoning, land suitability and land-use compatibility analysis. |
| Applied geosciences and geochemistry | Water, soil and sediment characterisation; geochemical and hydrogeological assessment. |
| Circular economy | Waste and material-flow assessments, resource recovery and economic analysis. |
| Environmental education | Training, awareness and community participation programmes tailored to the local context. |
| Logical framework project management | Problem assessment, objective and indicator formulation, planning and monitoring. |
| Urban planning and BIM design | Surveys, BIM modelling, coordination across disciplines and construction documentation. |

## Projects and experience

### Educational infrastructure facing flood risks

SWMM modelling and nature-based solutions

Research developed within the academic setting of Escuela Superior Politécnica del Litoral (ESPOL), with participation from team members. It is presented as the authors’ research experience, not as a project commissioned to or institutionally delivered by JACE Consulting.

[View project](https://jaceconsultingec.com/proyectos.html#proyecto-swmm) · [Technical note](proyectos/07-swmm-duran.md)

### Erosion and sediment transfer in the Daule basin

Spatial analysis using RUSLE–SDR

Research developed at ESPOL as part of Joao Andrés Calle Espinoza’s master’s thesis. It is included as the team member’s academic experience, not as a project commissioned to or institutionally delivered by JACE Consulting.

[View project](https://jaceconsultingec.com/proyectos.html#proyecto-rusle) · [Technical note](proyectos/01-daule-rusle-sdr.md)
