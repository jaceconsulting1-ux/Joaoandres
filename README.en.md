# Joao Calle Espinoza

**Hydrology · Geomatics · Environmental Data Science**

[Español](README.md) · [GitHub](https://github.com/Joaoandres) · [JACE Consulting](https://jaceconsultingec.com/)

I work at the intersection of hydrometeorological data analysis, catchment modelling, soil erosion and geospatial information to understand processes and support decisions about water, soil and land.

My projects combine **R and Python**, GIS analysis, interactive visualisation and scientific workflow automation. This portfolio brings together research and technical development associated with JACE Consulting and academic collaborations. Each project describes its available outputs, scope and limitations.

## Working approach

Define the question and spatial/temporal scale; check units, timestamps and quality; compare methods using physical and statistical criteria; distinguish observations from model estimates; communicate through code, maps and reproducible documentation.
